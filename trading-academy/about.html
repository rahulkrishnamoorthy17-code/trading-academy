About Trading Academy

India's most trusted stock market trading education platform since 2016

Our Story

Born from a Simple Belief: Every Indian Deserves Financial Literacy

Trading Academy was founded in 2016 by a group of SEBI-registered investment advisors and certified market professionals who noticed a glaring gap in India's financial education landscape.

Millions of retail investors were losing money in markets not because they lacked intelligence, but because they lacked structured, practical, and mentor-led education. We set out to fix that.

Today, with 10,000+ students across India, we've built the most comprehensive stock market trading curriculum available - combining theory, live market analysis, risk management, and psychological discipline.
