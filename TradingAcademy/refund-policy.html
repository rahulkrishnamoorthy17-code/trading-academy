Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This letter explains the general refund approach for eligible TradingAcademy course and subscription purchases.

7-Day Request Window

Eligible paid plans may be requested for refund within 7 calendar days of purchase, unless a specific product page clearly states different terms.

How to Request

Send your order or registered email details to our support team and briefly explain the request. We may ask for information needed to verify the purchase.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not apply where a substantial portion of downloadable or restricted content has been consumed, where misuse is identified, or where a product was clearly marked non-refundable before purchase, subject to applicable law.

Approved Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.

Course Transfers

Where appropriate, we may offer a course transfer or account credit as an alternative, but you may choose the eligible refund option available under the applicable terms.

Need Help?

If you are unsure whether your purchase is eligible, contact us with your order details and we will review it.
