Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This letter explains, in clear terms, how TradingAcademy handles information you provide while using our website, courses, enquiry forms and learning services.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, course preferences, enquiry details and basic technical information needed to operate and improve the website.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, manage registrations, provide course-related communication, improve our services and maintain website security. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments and Third-Party Services

Payment or sign-in services may be provided by trusted third parties. Their own privacy terms apply to information processed directly by those providers.

Cookies and Preferences

The website may store essential preferences such as theme, language direction or login-related settings in your browser to improve your experience.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information that we control, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
